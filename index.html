Connecting to the group…
Two things happen at the top of every hour: the marching band (06) comes down the City Hall ramp for 10 minutes, and the CN Tower lights up for Sources (72) for 30 minutes. The bar at the top counts down to the next one.
If you get tired, protect these: 09, 06, 15, 12, 05, 02. Cut in this order: Trinity Square, 01, 40 and 41, then Fort York Blvd.
Washrooms and food: the Snack & Skate booth west of the TORONTO sign closes its washrooms at 10 PM. After that, use the Event Centre on Bay just north of Queen, which also has food trucks and water refills.
Staying up all week: UFO (moving in front of Old City Hall), Blankets (Under) Cover and Echoes of the Apiary run until October 10. Everything else is tonight only.
Locations come from the City of Toronto's Nuit Blanche 2026 Travel Guide and art map. Information can change, so check toronto.ca/NuitBlanche if something isn't where it should be.
